## Campaign Strategy: AIMino on Hacker News

### The Product Angle

AIMino is a **self-hosted AI control center** that automates Slack, GitHub, and Gmail locally. On HN, the killer angles are:
- **Privacy-first** — your data never touches a third-party cloud
- **Self-hosted** — full control, no vendor lock-in
- **Developer-friendly** — appeals directly to HN's core audience
- **Replaces Zapier/Make** — but local, secure, and AI-native
- **Open source as marketing** — even if it's source-available or has a free tier, lead with the code

### Phase 1: Presence — Be a HN Citizen (Days 1-7)

Do NOT launch a product post first. HN punishes drive-by marketing. You earn the right to post.

**Daily actions (15 min/day):**
- Upvote thoughtful posts (don't overdo it)
- Write 1-2 quality comments in threads about: self-hosting, AI agents, dev tools, automation, privacy
- Bookmark relevant threads for later linking

**Comment strategy:**
- Add genuine value, not plugs. "I built something similar, here's what I learned about X"
- Reference AIMino naturally if relevant: "We're actually solving this with a self-hosted approach at aimino.de"
- Use your real experience. You're the founder — that's authority.

### Phase 2: The "Tell HN" / "Ask HN" Gambit (Days 7-10)

Choose ONE of these approaches:

**Option A: "Ask HN: What's stopping you from self-hosting your AI tools?"**
- Type: Ask HN
- Purpose: Market research disguised as community discussion
- Engagement driver: Everyone has opinions on self-hosting
- Follow-up: In comments, position AIMino as the answer to the pain points surfaced

**Option B: "Show HN: I built a self-hosted AI agent that manages my dev workflow — Slack, GitHub, email"**
- Type: Show HN
- Purpose: Product launch with "builder" framing
- Hook: "Built this because I didn't want my team's data feeding into cloud AI APIs"
- Content: Brief, technical, honest. Share what it does, how it works, what's hard
- CTA: "Would love feedback — here's the demo/waitlist"

### Phase 3: Deep Engagement (Days 10-21)

- Reply to EVERY comment on your Show HN/Ask HN
- Be humble, technical, and open to criticism
- If someone asks "how is this different from CrewAI?" — have a crisp, honest answer
- If someone finds a flaw, thank them and say how you'll fix it
- HN respects founders who engage authentically

**Seeding play — getting others to mention AIMino:**
- Reach out to relevant HN users (check their profiles for blogs/newsletters)
- Offer early access in exchange for honest technical feedback
- If someone writes a blog post about self-hosted AI → comment with value + mention AIMino as an option

### Phase 4: Second Wave — The Deeper Post (Day 21+)

Once you have some HN karma and maybe a successful Show HN:

**Post idea: "Why I'm building a self-hosted alternative to every SaaS AI tool"**
- Narrative post (not Show HN, just a story/opinion)
- Weave in AIMino as the natural conclusion
- HN loves founder stories that are genuine, not salesy

**Or technical deep-dive: "Building a self-hosted AI agent: the architecture decisions I had to make"**
- Very technical, very honest
- Gets upvoted by engineers who appreciate the depth
- Links to AIMino naturally

### Success Metrics

- **Show HN:** 50+ upvotes, 30+ comments = solid launch
- **Ask HN:** 80+ comments = strong engagement
- **Waitlist signups from HN:** 30-100 in first week after Show HN
- **Karma growth:** Enough to post future content without restrictions
- **Brand awareness:** HN users who haven't heard of AIMino → "oh that self-hosted AI thing from HN"

### Don't Do List

- ❌ Post "We're building the future of enterprise AI!" — too vague, too hype
- ❌ Link-dump in comments — HN mods will flag you
- ❌ Argue with critics — you lose either way
- ❌ Post more than once every 7-10 days — you'll get flagged as a spammer
- ❌ Use GPT-sounding language — be human, be raw
- ❌ Upvote your own posts from alt accounts — instant ban

### Toolkit

- **HN Profile:** Set your profile to include aimino.de and a brief bio. HN users check profiles.
- **Alerts:** Use hnhiring.me or similar to track mentions of "AI agent", "self-hosted", "automation"
- **Timing:** Best posting times: 8-10 AM ET (14-16 CET) on weekdays for maximum visibility
