# Verified Publisher Application Guide

> Step-by-step guide to apply for the **Verified Publisher** badge on GitHub Marketplace.

---

## Overview

The Verified Publisher badge helps users trust that your app is published by a verified organization. GitHub requires organization verification before you can publish to Marketplace.

---

## Prerequisites

- [ ] A **GitHub Organization** (not a personal account)
- [ ] Organization **verified domain** (see steps below)
- [ ] Organization **two-factor authentication** (2FA) enabled
- [ ] **Marketplace listing** already drafted or submitted
- [ ] **Privacy policy URL** live and accessible
- [ ] **Terms of service URL** live and accessible
- [ ] **Logo** prepared (120×120 PNG)
- [ ] **Screenshots** prepared (1280×640 PNG)

---

## Step 1: Verify Your Organization's Domain

1. Go to your organization's **Settings** → **Third-party access** → **Domain verification**
2. Add your domain (e.g. `aimino.io`)
3. Follow GitHub's DNS verification instructions (add a TXT record to your DNS provider)
4. Wait for DNS propagation (can take up to 24 hours, typically ~5 minutes)
5. Click **Verify** — the domain will show as "Verified"

> 📖 **Docs:** [Verifying your organization's domain](https://docs.github.com/en/organizations/managing-organization-settings/verifying-your-organizations-domain)

---

## Step 2: Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

All organization members with admin access must have 2FA enabled.

1. Go to **Organization settings** → **Security** → **Authentication security**
2. Enforce 2FA for all members
3. Confirm that all members have set up 2FA

> 📖 **Docs:** [Requiring two-factor authentication in your organization](https://docs.github.com/en/organizations/keeping-your-organization-secure/managing-two-factor-authentication-for-your-organization/requiring-two-factor-authentication-in-your-organization)

---

## Step 3: Complete Organization Profile

1. Go to **Organization settings** → **Profile**
2. Fill in:
   - **Display name** — e.g. "AImino"
   - **Short description** — e.g. "AI infrastructure for developers"
   - **Location** — optional but recommended
   - **Website URL** — e.g. `https://aimino.io`
   - **Email** — public support email
3. Upload an **organization avatar** (square, at least 200×200 px)

---

## Step 4: Submit the Verified Publisher Application

1. Go to **GitHub Marketplace** → **Publisher settings**
   - URL: `https://github.com/marketplace/manage/publishers`
2. Click **"Apply for publisher verification"**
3. Complete the application form:
   - Organization name
   - Contact email
   - Domain verification confirmation
   - Agree to Marketplace terms
4. Submit

> 📖 **Docs:** [Applying for publisher verification](https://docs.github.com/en/apps/github-marketplace/github-marketplace-overview/applying-for-publisher-verification-for-your-organization)

---

## Step 5: Wait for Review

- GitHub's review typically takes **3–5 business days**
- You'll receive an email notification when the review is complete
- If rejected, the email will include reasons and steps to reapply

---

## Post-Verification Checklist

- [ ] ✅ Domain verified
- [ ] ✅ 2FA enforced
- [ ] ✅ Organization profile complete
- [ ] ✅ Application submitted
- [ ] ✅ Badge received
- [ ] ✅ Publish Marketplace listing
