**Tweet 4/6**
Most valuable lesson: the Patch tier (patch_html + set_attribute) was the surprise MVP.

I built this thinking Assembly (render_page) would be the headline feature. Turns out developers really value surgical editing — letting AI read existing HTML, understand it, and make targeted changes without regenerating everything.

**Tweet 5/6**
What I'd do differently:
• Launch with custom component support (#1 feature request)
• Submit to MCP directories on Day 1
• Record a 30s demo video before launch
• Start Discord engagement weeks earlier
